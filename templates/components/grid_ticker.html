Grid alert levels

Declared by NGCP as System Operator, separately for the Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao grids. ARECO is on the Visayas grid.

Normal (white)

Supply is enough for all demand and the required reserves are available. No interruptions expected.

Blue alert

Precaution: a typhoon or other calamity is expected within about 24 hours and may damage power plants or transmission lines.

Yellow alert

The operating margin (spare capacity of running plants) is below the grid's contingency requirement. Supply still meets demand, so no brownouts, but another plant tripping could push the grid to red.

Red alert

Supply is not enough to meet demand plus the grid's regulating requirement. Manual load dropping (rotating brownouts) by distribution utilities may follow.

How the status here is set: a notice like "Visayas Grid is on YELLOW Alert for the interval hours 1800H-2000H" shows as today before the window and now (pulsing) during it, until the window ends or a "lifted" notice arrives. Otherwise the latest NSO grid update's "System Condition" is used.

    Source: NGCP System Operator advisories via the IEMOP real-time market dashboard. Checked every 30 seconds; IEMOP itself publishes in batches, often 30–60 minutes after the advisory time.
